## APPENDIX B: SCORING METHODOLOGY

### Composite Score Formula
```
Composite Score = (Freq_Score × 0.35) + (Price_Score × 0.25) + 
                  (Competition_Score × 0.20) + (Position_Score × 0.20)
```

### Component Scores (0-100 scale)

**1. Frequency Score (35% weight)**
- Normalized against max frequency in dataset
- Linear scale: `(route_freq / max_freq) × 100`
- Higher = More search demand

**2. Price Score (25% weight)**
- Log-normalized to reduce outlier impact
- Formula: `(log(route_price + 1) / log(max_price + 1)) × 100`
- Higher = More revenue per conversion

**3. Competition Score (20% weight)**
- Inverse scale (lower competition = higher score)
- Formula: `100 - ((route_competition / max_competition) × 100)`
- Higher = Cheaper CPC expected

**4. Position Score (20% weight)**
- **Not ranking:** 100 points (Direct most valuable)
- **Ranking 11-20:** 50-95 points (Direct still valuable)
- **Ranking 1-10:** 0-50 points (Direct less critical)
- Formula: `100 - (position × 5)` or `100` if not ranking

### Why These Weights?

- **Freq (35%):** Search volume is the strongest predictor of total revenue opportunity
- **Price (25%):** Higher tickets = more forgiving CPC economics
- **Competition (20%):** Directly impacts CPC and profitability
- **Position (20%):** Determines urgency of Direct need

### Score Interpretation

| Score Range | Interpretation | Action |
|-------------|----------------|--------|
| 80-100 | **Exceptional** | Launch immediately, high budget |
| 65-79 | **Excellent** | Launch in Wave 1, medium-high budget |
| 50-64 | **Good** | Launch in Wave 2, medium budget |
| 35-49 | **Moderate** | Launch in Wave 3, test budget |
